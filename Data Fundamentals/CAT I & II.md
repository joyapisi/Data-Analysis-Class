## 4. CAT I and CAT II — 30 points

The CATs should assess students' foundational understanding of data careers and their ability to interpret and work with datasets.

## 15

points

PART I: Data Fundamentals and Careers

Suggested duration: 30 minutes

- Explain the responsibilities of a data analyst. (3 points)
- Differentiate between a dataset, a variable, a record, and a data type. (3 points)
- Identify suitable tools for an Excel-based analysis and an R-based analysis. (3 points)
- Given a dataset, identify its rows, columns, and variables. (3 points)
- Interpret a simple chart and state two findings. (3 points)

## 15

points

PART II: Practical Data Analysis

Suggested duration: 40–50 minutes

- Import or open a supplied dataset and identify its variables. (2 points)
- Calculate a relevant summary statistic. (3 points)
- Identify a trend, comparison, or unusual observation. (3 points)
- Create an appropriate chart using Excel/Sheets or R and ggplot2. (4 points)
- Write a short evidence-based conclusion. (3 points)
