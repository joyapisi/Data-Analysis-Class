## 3. Individual assignment — 10 points

Title: E-Commerce Sales Performance Analysis

Individual practical · Excel or Google Sheets

Scenario: An online retailer wants to know which products perform best, which categories generate the most revenue and how sales vary across transactions.

### Student tasks

Using a dummy e-commerce sales dataset:
1. Calculate total revenue using **quantity** and **unit price**. (2 points)
2. Identify the **best-performing** product or category. (2 points)
3. Identify the **worst-performing** product or category. (2 points)
   
Total: 10 points

### Submission requirements

- Excel or Google Sheet file with two sheets.
- Sheet 1 should hold the original dummy data.
- Sheet 2 should show the original dummy data and the best + worst performing products/categories.
