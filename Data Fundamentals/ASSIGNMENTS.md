# Data Fundamentals: Continuous Assessment Plan

Course focus: Careers in Data, data analysis workflows, data visualization, and the use of tools such as Excel, Google Sheets, Posit Cloud, R and ggplot2.

Total assessment: 55 points

Since students have explored two Data Analyst career pathways through practical projects, the assessments should test both their understanding of careers in data and their ability to apply the tools to real-world datasets.

Title: Data Analysts at Work — Turning Data into Decisions

Groups of 3–5 students · Practical project

Scenario: A research organization wants to understand economic performance in selected African countries. Your team has been hired to analyze GDP data, identify trends and communicate findings through visualizations.

### Student tasks

1. Identify the role of a data analyst in the project and explain the business question being investigated.
2. Obtain a suitable GDP dataset covering multiple African countries and years.
3. Import the dataset into Posit Cloud.
4. Inspect the dataset and identify the variables, their data types and any data-quality issues.
5. Use R to summarize GDP trends across selected countries.
6. Create at least two visualizations using `ggplot2`, including a time-series line chart.
7. Write three evidence-based findings from the analysis.
8. Present recommendations or conclusions supported by the data.

### Marking rubric

| Criterion                           | points |
| ----------------------------------- | ----- |
| Career role and research question   | 2     |
| Dataset selection and understanding | 2     |
| Data inspection and preparation     | 3     |
| Correct R analysis and summaries    | 3     |
| Visualizations using ggplot2        | 3     |
| Interpretation and presentation     | 2     |
| Total                               | 15    |

Deliverables: R script or R Markdown file, dataset source, two visualizations and a short report of findings.

Title: E-Commerce Sales Performance Analysis

Individual practical · Excel or Google Sheets

Scenario: An online retailer wants to know which products perform best, which categories generate the most revenue and how sales vary across transactions.

### Student tasks

Using a supplied e-commerce sales dataset:

1. Identify the dataset's rows, columns and key variables. (2 points)
2. Calculate total revenue using quantity and unit price. (2 points)
3. Identify the best-performing product or category using sorting, filtering or summary calculations. (2 points)
4. Create a chart that communicates a sales performance finding. (2 points)
5. Write two insights that a business could use to make a decision. (2 points)

Total: 10 points

### Submission requirements

- Excel or Google Sheets file.
- At least one chart.
- A short interpretation of the results.

Students should explain what the data reveals rather than simply submit calculations.

## 4. CAT I and CAT II — 30 points

The CATs should assess students' foundational understanding of data careers and their ability to interpret and work with datasets.

## 15

points

PART I: Data Fundamentals and Careers

Suggested duration: 30 minutes

- Explain the responsibilities of a data analyst. (3 points)
- Differentiate between a dataset, a variable, a record and a data type. (3 points)
- Identify suitable tools for an Excel-based analysis and an R-based analysis. (3 points)
- Given a dataset, identify its rows, columns and variables. (3 points)
- Interpret a simple chart and state two findings. (3 points)

## 15

points

PART II: Practical Data Analysis

Suggested duration: 40–50 minutes

- Import or open a supplied dataset and identify its variables. (2 points)
- Calculate a relevant summary statistic. (3 points)
- Identify a trend, comparison, or unusual observation. (3 points)
- Create an appropriate chart using Excel/Sheets or R and ggplot2. (4 points)
- Write a short evidence-based conclusion. (3 points)
